## How to use this

When a spec is planned, check this ledger for its section. Every item listed
against it must appear in that feature's `plan.md` — either scheduled, or
explicitly deferred with a reason. **An item may be moved later. It may not
quietly disappear.**

If something here has no home, or is in the wrong one, say so and it gets fixed.
